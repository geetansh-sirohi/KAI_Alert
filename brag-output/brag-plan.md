# Brag Plan: KAI Alert

## What is this app?
KAI Alert is an autonomous 4D spatio-temporal disaster command platform that predicts civil infrastructure failure—hospital generator drownings, power substation collapse, and bridge submergence—hours ahead of cyclone landfall.

## The angle
Standard meteorological warnings announce wind speeds and atmospheric pressure, but leave emergency teams blind to structural reality. KAI Alert turns raw vector tracks and 30m digital elevation models into actionable physical failure forecasts and dynamic evacuation routes.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-contrast kinetic typography: "WEATHER SAYS 165 KM/H CYCLONE COMING. BUT WHAT ACTUALLY BREAKS?" followed by an instant pulse into KAI Alert's live command telemetry.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **4D Atmospheric & Coastal Surge Engine:** Live telemetry cards snapping in (165 km/h wind, 950 hPa pressure, +3.2m storm surge).
- **Physical Failure Forecast:** Hospital ICU generator drowning alert + 132kV substation trip warning.
- **Dynamic Evacuation Detour & Drone Triage:** Real-time routing diverting civilian convoys away from submerged bridges while multimodal AI assesses aerial drone damage.

## Outro / punchline
"Don't just watch the radar. Protect the grid. KAI Alert — Autonomous Disaster Intelligence."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Sensor & GPS Sync:** Real-time location & atmospheric telemetry locking in.
2. **Deterministic Storm Simulation:** Simulating Cyclone Dana's 4D landfall vector.
3. **Emergency Action:** Critical infrastructure breach detected → Evacuation detour dynamically plotted → Vernacular SMS dispatched.
