## 💼 LinkedIn
Standard meteorology forecasts wind speeds and atmospheric pressure drops. But for civil defense teams, atmospheric data doesn't answer the operational question: **What actually breaks?**

That’s why we built **KAI Alert** — a 4D spatio-temporal civil vulnerability command system.

Instead of generic radar maps, KAI Alert predicts physical infrastructure collapse:
1. **Critical Healthcare:** Forecasts which hospital basement backup diesel generators will drown in coastal storm surge, cutting ICU power.
2. **Dynamic Evacuation Detours:** Identifies low-elevation bridge submergence hours in advance and reroutes convoys to high ground.
3. **Multimodal Aerial Recon:** Feeds post-impact drone imagery into Gemini 2.0 to instantly assess structural damage.
4. **Resilient Communications:** Dispatches ultra-low-bandwidth emergency alerts (<140 bytes) in Odia, Hindi, and Bengali over mesh radio.

Built with Next.js 15, Leaflet 4D GIS, Turf.js, and deterministic civil physics equations.
